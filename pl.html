The Project

Project Overview

The Challenge

The Premier League data was available in individual season datasets , each with row level match data , however as the years progressed the amount of metrics increased , with earlier seasons containing just the base metrics. The first season however was missing and could not be included in the project.

The goal was to combine all sources into one common analytical dataset and use this to identify interesting stats and trends across various seasons of Premier League football and see how they compare.

What the Data Shows

Comparing different Premier League eras , we can see an increase each era in the Goals scored per game , winning margins , and Away win % showing us that the emphasis on home games has decreased and overall there are more goals being scored compared to the 90’s and 00’s

We can also compare individual team performance across seasons to see trends such as points needed to be champions and what points total it now takes to survive relegation compared to previous seasons.

Process

How I Built It

The project followed an end-to-end analytical workflow from raw official data through to interactive reporting.

01

Data

Collected yearly match data from 35 individual datasets.

02

Cleaning

Standardised fields, extracted reporting year and week, flagged missing data and combined the datasets using Power Query.

03

Analysis

Used SQL, Excel and DAX to investigate annual trends, growth, team performance and season performance

04

Built a multi-page Power BI report to communicate the findings through interactive visualisations.

Problem Solving

Technical Challenge: Home & Away Team Analysis

!

The Challenge

Each match contains both a home team and an away team. Team-level comparisons and head-to-head analysis therefore require logic that accounts for both sides of a fixture without excluding away matches or double-counting results.

✓

The Approach

Used the match table alongside team and season dimensions, with DAX calculations for home and away results, points per game, and independent team selections for head-to-head comparisons.

Built using SQL Server, Power BI and DAX.

Methodology

Data Quality & Limitations

Historical comparisons need consistent definitions and careful treatment of incomplete or unavailable data

Missing Early Season

Missing the first Premier League season so my data only starts at 93/94 season

2026 Partial Year

2026/2027 is incomplete as this season is currently in progress so all starts at season to date.

Missing Values

The early year data sets do not contain all match information such as cards, corners and attendances so cannot use these as comparison points.

Fair Era Comparisons

Used per-match or per-game rates where appropriate, since era lengths and league match counts can differ..
